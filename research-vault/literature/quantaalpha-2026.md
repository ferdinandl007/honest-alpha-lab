# QuantaAlpha: An Evolutionary Framework for LLM-Driven Alpha Mining

Source: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07085v1) · [author repository](https://github.com/QuantaAlpha/QuantaAlpha).

## Evidence reviewed

Mutates and recombines research trajectories, with hypothesis-to-code consistency, complexity and redundancy controls. The reviewed split trains on 2016–2020, validates on 2021 and tests 2022–December 2025; transfer experiments include S&P500.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

After a simple evolutionary baseline works, test whether changing the full research trajectory adds value beyond changing formulas alone.

## Limits and remaining checks

Historical tests preceding the research/model release are not guaranteed free from pretrained knowledge contamination. Results remain unreplicated here. Keep generation, model fitting and trajectory-selection budgets comparable.
