# Lazy Prices

Source: [primary source](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25084).

## Evidence reviewed

Cohen, Malloy and Nguyen link changes in corporate reports to subsequent operations and returns in a historical US sample. The 2018 working paper was published in the Journal of Finance in 2020.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Treat issuer-relative disclosure changes as a replication baseline. Test whether structured semantic changes improve on cheap text-difference measures on modern, timestamp-correct filings.

## Limits and remaining checks

This is a long-known signal family, not an original discovery by our agents. Historical efficacy does not establish current alpha after publication, costs or competing factors. The original full empirical specification was not replicated.
