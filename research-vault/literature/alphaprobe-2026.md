# AlphaPROBE: Alpha Mining via Principled Retrieval and On-graph Biased Evolution

Source: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11917v1) · [author repository](https://github.com/gta0804/AlphaPROBE).

## Evidence reviewed

Stores factor ancestry in a graph and balances parent quality against repeated exploration and diversity. The reviewed Chinese-equity setup uses 20-day targets, training through 2020, validation to June 2022 and testing July 2022–June 2025.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Add derived-from, duplicates and fails-because links to the vault; retrieve informative failures as well as successful parents.

## Limits and remaining checks

The numerical-diversity expressions include aggregations where signed correlations can cancel. Our adaptation should use sign-invariant pairwise similarity and actual marginal contribution. A posterior-inspired retrieval score is not a calibrated probability of profitable alpha.
