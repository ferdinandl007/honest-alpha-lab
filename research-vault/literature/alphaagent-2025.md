# AlphaAgent: LLM-Driven Alpha Mining with Regularized Exploration to Counteract Alpha Decay

Source: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.16789v1).

## Evidence reviewed

Combines idea generation, implementation and evaluation with penalties for complexity, weak hypothesis alignment and overlap with a reference alpha collection. Formula-tree comparisons help constrain repetitive search.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Require an economic hypothesis and executable expression; canonicalize formulas before reserving expensive evaluation jobs.

## Limits and remaining checks

Syntactic novelty does not demonstrate independent information. A rewritten, sign-flipped or rescaled factor can still reproduce a known exposure. Financial acceptance must use numerical evidence, not the agent's rationale.
