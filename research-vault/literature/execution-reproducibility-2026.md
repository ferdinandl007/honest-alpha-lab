# Beyond Agent Architecture: Execution Assumptions and Reproducibility in LLM-Based Trading Systems

Source: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08285v1).

## Evidence reviewed

Audits 30 trading-related studies for information timing, splits, costs, turnover, execution, universe and artifacts. Its worked equity example is a methodological scaffold, not production validation.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Make execution assumptions and artifact completeness part of benchmark eligibility. Reject reports that cannot reconstruct prices, orders, costs and the eligible universe.

## Limits and remaining checks

The bounded audit is not a verdict on all agentic-finance research. It reinforces the need to repair our evaluation and portfolio pipeline before interpreting mined candidates as strategies.
