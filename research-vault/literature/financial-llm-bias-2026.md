# Evaluating LLMs in Finance Requires Explicit Bias Consideration

Source: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14233v1).

## Evidence reviewed

This review/position paper identifies look-ahead, survivorship, narrative, objective and cost biases across financial-LLM work. It distinguishes information leakage through model parameters from leakage through external retrieval.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Record model/prompt versions, historical source snapshots and retrieval provenance. Audit publication eligibility before any reward. Begin prospective feature collection after freezing the extraction procedure.

## Limits and remaining checks

A declared model cutoff or date-filtered search is not a complete information barrier. Current search rankings and updated pages can leak later context. This paper diagnoses methodology; it does not prove a profitable mining architecture.
