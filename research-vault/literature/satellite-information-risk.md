# Is Information Risk Priced? New Evidence from Outer Space

Source: [primary source](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2023.00713).

## Evidence reviewed

The publisher abstract connects US-retailer parking-lot imagery to earnings information, and studies cloud-related information asymmetry and subsequent returns. Online publication was December 2024.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Separate what a proxy measures, who can observe it, and how it might affect prices. For retail demand, verify the operating/earnings bridge and then test incremental residual returns.

## Limits and remaining checks

This is evidence about a specific economic mechanism, not an autonomous dataset creator. Satellite access and historical observations are not supplied by the paper; licensing, coverage and delayed observation remain independent requirements.
