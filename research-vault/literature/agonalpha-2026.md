# AgonAlpha: Autonomous Alpha Discovery via Prompt Economy and Scalable Agentic Search

Source: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11250v1) · [author repository](https://github.com/AutoResearch-Factory/AgonAlpha).

## Evidence reviewed

Searches frozen research artifacts with a fresh-context reviewer and pending-aware scheduling. The paper describes WorldQuant BRAIN US-equity experiments on a fixed 2019–2023 benchmark and reviewer interventions against inconsistent evidence.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Have a separate critic check that reported metrics belong to the exact expression, data snapshot and run. Deterministic services, not the critic, retain financial acceptance authority.

## Limits and remaining checks

Repeated external evaluation does not eliminate selection overfitting. Although the paper describes a public evidence trail, the inspected repository releases orchestration and excludes local candidates/results and evaluator integration. Full reproduction was not established; the packaged workflow is not a tested drop-in Codex integration.
