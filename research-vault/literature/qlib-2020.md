# Qlib: An AI-oriented Quantitative Investment Platform

Source: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.11189) · [author repository](https://github.com/microsoft/qlib).

## Evidence reviewed

Provides reusable interfaces for quantitative data processing, models and evaluation. The maintained Microsoft repository also points to R&D-Agent automation.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Use an adapter to a fixed conventional model/data workflow. Compare our panel operations against independently implemented Qlib-compatible expectations.

## Limits and remaining checks

A framework does not supply a licensed, historically correct US security universe or guarantee point-in-time fundamentals. Integration compatibility and performance have not been tested in this review.
