# AlphaBench: Benchmarking Large Language Models in Formulaic Alpha Factor Mining

Source: [primary source](https://alphabench.cc/) · [author repository](https://github.com/CityU-MLO/AlphaBench).

## Evidence reviewed

The ICLR 2026 author project separates generation, zero-shot evaluation and iterative search, using Qlib-compatible execution. It reports evolutionary search outperforming the compared experience/tree approaches and weak LLM-only factor judging on its benchmarks.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Reuse the task separation and search baselines. Measure execution validity, useful unique discoveries, numerical quality and cost separately.

## Limits and remaining checks

The paper/PDF access was blocked by a browser challenge; this note relies on the author project and repository, not a full-paper audit. Its CSI300/S&P500 setup does not establish a universally best search method or model.
