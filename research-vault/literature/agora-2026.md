# AI Trading's Alpha Singularity: Emergent Market Reasoning through Agent-to-Agent Self-Evolution

Source: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29194v1).

## Evidence reviewed

Agora explores jointly evolving research artifacts and evaluation-related skills under typed information boundaries. Its principal CSI1000 result uses a single full-system seed and a 91-trading-day 2026 holdout; returns are short-side concentrated.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Retain explicit role boundaries and evaluator disagreement as audit evidence. Track as a research comparison, not an immediate replacement architecture.

## Limits and remaining checks

Its PPO relay is outside phase 1. We will not permit agents to change final acceptance or sealed tests. Short holdouts and unequal seed replication cannot establish superiority for our portfolio; a long-short result is not a long-only result.
