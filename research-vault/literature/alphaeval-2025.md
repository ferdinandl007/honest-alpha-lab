# AlphaEval: A Comprehensive and Efficient Evaluation Framework for Formula Alpha Mining

Source: [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.13174v1).

## Evidence reviewed

Organizes factor assessment around predictive power, stability, robustness, financial logic and diversity. Its backtest-free framing avoids a full portfolio simulation, not the need for observed market data and outcomes.

## Application to Honest Alpha Lab — our recommendation

Use inexpensive diagnostics to screen development candidates before cost-sensitive portfolio simulation. Retain the individual measurements rather than only a composite grade.

## Limits and remaining checks

A screening score cannot establish executable profit or replace a sealed test. Economic plausibility may support interpretation, but it must not override numerical failures. This review did not verify a runnable official repository.
