# Research round 001 — public-data discovery

Date: 2026-09-01

This is the first source-backed research intake generated by separate symbolic, text/event, and alternative-data research workers. It is not a backtest, not an approved dataset, and not a trading recommendation. No candidate below may enter the sealed test or be marked accepted until it has immutable raw inputs, a point-in-time replay, and independent numerical validation.

## Shared acceptance rules

- Persist original releases/files, retrieval time, source URL, content hash, parser version, and market-availability timestamp.
- Treat the source publication/filing timestamp as `available_at`; never use the economic observation date as a substitute.
- Use purged walk-forward testing on 5-, 10-, 20-, and 60-trading-day sector- and beta-residual returns.
- Reject candidates that fail incremental-information, similarity, stability, cost, and regime-conditioned checks.

## First two ingestion spikes

1. **EIA WPSR:** best initial alternative-data ingestion because it has a precise release clock, compact tabular artifacts, a long history, and clear sector pathways.
2. **SEC Forms 4:** best initial text/event ingestion because the source is public, structured, time-stamped, and has a defined event taxonomy.

Both remain `DISCOVERED`/`PROPOSED`. The next work is an immutable historical-vintage download, schema mapping, availability-time replay, and purged out-of-sample evaluation—never a live trade.
